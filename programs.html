G A G Systems, Architecture
G A G Systems is a collection of many Small webprograms that each solves few important problems for computer users on the web/internet

As an Architecture, it is a polylithic system, it gets bigger as its small webprograms increase..In contrast, a monolithic system is one big program that gets bigger by making a bigger version of itself

Benefits: small webprograms are easier to maintain,test,and enhance, programming time is greatly reduced,cost of making these webprograms is low, and price of sale of these webprograms is cheap.
